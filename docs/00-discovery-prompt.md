## What it covers

- Role: brand strategist, product manager and senior web engineer in one interviewer; asks, challenges and records; no designing during the interview.
- Who you are interviewing: Jigar's credentials (RYT-200 in progress, CPT, RADT trainee, weekly trauma-informed vinyasa in a treatment-center setting), what exists (no site; Free Body park class launching Dec 6, 2026; class names TIDE, Still Dance, Layers, Nataraja Five Acts; sequencing app planned; Métier as umbrella), values (love, spirituality, self-discovery, healing, growth, wholistic fitness across mental, physical, spiritual, emotional, intellectual, social), voice rules (precision and restraint, no genre vocabulary, no em dashes), the Free Body visual seed (paper #E9EFE2, cell #F3F6EF, line #9DB896, ink #1F2B27, red #B5392A; Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono), and the known gaps (early hours, scope of practice, unbuilt business infrastructure, overcommitment).
- Rules of engagement: one phase per turn, four questions max, defaults in brackets, challenge vagueness and "both", values translated into observable behavior, a scope-of-practice guard, a running decision log and parking lot, testable requirements, three sittings with resume summaries.
- Phases 0 to 12: context check; job of the site and the one number; brand architecture and name (him vs a brand vs Métier; class names as products); values made operational (six dimensions as compass or framework; the wholistic spelling; three voice samples); audiences and fears; offers and money (park class, PT, privates, workshops, consulting, coaching behind the guard, writing, the app); content and the Instagram loop; site map; functional requirements with MoSCoW; technical decisions as ADRs (Astro default, hosting, phone-editable schedule, forms and waiver PII, booking and payments, email, analytics, images, design system from Free Body tokens, relation to the sequencing app, operations); legal and scope statements; measurement; v0 before Dec 6, v1 personal training, v2 writing and sequences, hours budget, kill criteria.
- Outputs: brand brief, audience cards, offer sheet, site map, requirements table, ADRs, content inventory, legal checklist, metrics, versions, risks, decision log, open questions, and a self-contained build brief.
